# Framepilot downloads

Framepilot makes films with **Google Flow** from your desktop: characters, key frames, video clips, narration,
the final cut and captions, in one free app. It uses Flow's own credits (50 free every day with any Google
account).

## Download

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/skorkoyah/framepilot-releases/releases/latest) and pick your file:

| Computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) | `framepilot-<version>-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `framepilot-<version>-x64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11 | `framepilot-<version>-x64-setup.exe` |

You also need [Google Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/). Everything else is inside the app, and it updates
itself.

## Good to know

Framepilot drives Flow's website for you, in its own Chrome window signed in as you. It can stop working when
Google changes Flow, and automated use may be against Google's terms of service. Use it on your own account.

This repository holds the installers only.
